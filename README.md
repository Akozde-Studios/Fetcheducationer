# FEHT
The actual name of this local AI language model is 'FEHT'—an acronym standing for 'Fetcher, Educationer, Hyper, Technology.' It is a versatile model that operates in an eco-friendly manner, relying solely on software architectures. The project is independent and does not transmit user data to any third parties. It primarily trains itself during conversations via websites that utilize the 'MediaWiki' infrastructure. Rather than relying on existing open-source code or standard AI APIs, it is developed using 'GDevelop Block 5' and 'JavaScript.' It is designed to facilitate easier access to online information, image generation and editing, songwriting, and the exchange of information in both Turkish and foreign languages. There will be no paid add-ons for advanced features, nor will there be any daily usage limits. It is anticipated that the Republic of Turkey will play a significant role in the processes surrounding this local AI software. As a versatile local AI language model, it inherently carries the potential to make errors. The 'Akozde Studios' team and lead developer Necati Rüzgar Özdemir bear no responsibility for any statements generated by the model—or resulting from its internet integration—that might infringe upon the values ​​of others. It is designed to adapt to your needs and clarify its own reasoning. The model currently known as 'FEHT' is in the early access phase of its initial version. In line with the aforementioned eco-friendly approach, it technically transforms your computer into its own server. It has no political affiliations, and its use in specific political situations is not recommended. To log in, you must accept our terms, policies, and security measures.
